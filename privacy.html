Privacy Policy — 547 Dashboard

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Who uses this app

547 Dashboard is a private tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels and Facebook pages. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

  • YouTube data of the owner's own channels: video metadata, channel and video analytics, and comments on the owner's videos (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API).
  • Uploading and scheduling videos, and posting comment replies, on the owner's own channels.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to plan, schedule and analyse the owner's own content.
  • OAuth tokens and cached statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared or transferred to third parties.
  • The app does not access accounts other than the owner's.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is handled in accordance with the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions.
